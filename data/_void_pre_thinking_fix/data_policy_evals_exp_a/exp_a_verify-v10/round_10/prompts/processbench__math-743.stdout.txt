**Using Lagrange multipliers:**
- $\nabla f = (2x, 2y)$ where $f = x^2 + y^2$
- $g = x^2 + 8xy + 7y^2 - 225$

**System:**
$2x = \lambda(2x + 8y)$
$2y = \lambda(8x + 14y)$

From these: $\frac{2x}{2y} = \frac{2x + 8y}{8x + 14y}$

$x(8x + 14y) = y(2x + 8y)$
$8x^2 + 14xy = 2xy + 8y^2$
$8x^2 + 12xy - 8y^2 = 0$
$2x^2 + 3xy - 2y^2 = 0$

**Factoring:** $(2x - y)(x + 2y) = 0$
