By symmetry, when two congruent equilateral triangles are positioned to form a Star of David configuration on a regular hexagon, the overlap is a smaller regular hexagon whose area is exactly 2/3 of each triangle's area.

Intersection area = $\frac{2}{3} \times 12\sqrt{3} = 8\sqrt{3}$ square inches.
