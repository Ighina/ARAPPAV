**Key insight about parity:**
A switch is ON if it has been flipped an odd number of times.
A switch is OFF if it has been flipped an even number of times (including 0).

For all 6 switches to be ON, each must be flipped an odd number of times.

**Total flips across all minutes:**
If we take m minutes and flip 3 switches per minute, the total number of flips is 3m.

**Parity constraint:**
For all 6 switches to be ON, the sum of flip counts must be such that each switch has an odd flip count. The minimum odd count is 1 flip per switch, giving a total of 6 flips minimum.

However, we must flip exactly 3m switches total over m minutes (counting multiplicity).

So 3m must equal the total number of individual flips across all switches.

**Can we do it in 1 minute?**

- We'd need all 6 switches ON, requiring at least 6 flips total (one per switch)

Therefore E(6,3) = 2.

**Calculate I(6,3):**
I(6,3) = E(6,3) - 6/3 = 2 - 2 = 0
